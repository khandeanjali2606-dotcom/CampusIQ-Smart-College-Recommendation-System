Where this data comes from, and how to read it

Maharashtra: institutes come from the official CAP 2026-27 list and are placed in districts by their address. Branches marked “cross-checked” come from the official seat matrix; others show typical branches — open the “Official CAP page” link to confirm. Districts with no CAP-listed engineering college (for example Gadchiroli, Gondia, Hingoli) fall back to nearby districts.

Institutions, districts and branches. Colleges marked “branch listing cross-checked” were matched against published listings (links below) on 14 Oct 2026. Others are well-known institutions whose branch lists were not re-checked here — confirm on the college site.

Fees. “Official notice” = taken from the college’s own 2025-26 fee notice. “Reported” = figure shown by an education aggregator, which can be stale. “Estimate” = a typical band for that kind of institute, not a quote. Always confirm the fee for your category (CAP / institute-level / management).

Required percentile. An indicative figure by selectivity tier, not an official cutoff. Real cutoffs change every year, branch and category — check the official CAP / JoSAA / college closing lists.
